Cheat sheet

der

  • Male people (der Vater, der Lehrer)
  • Days, parts of the day, months, seasons — except die Nacht
  • Most weather: der Regen, der Schnee, der Wind
  • -ling, -ismus, -ist

die

  • Female people (die Mutter, die Lehrerin)
  • -ung, -heit, -keit, -schaft, -ion, -tät
  • -ik, -ur, -ei, -ie
  • Most nouns ending in -e (die Lampe, die Schule)

das

  • -chen, -lein (always!)
  • -um (das Museum), -ment (das Instrument)
  • Young beings: das Kind, das Baby
  • Most “ge-” collectives and infinitives used as nouns (das Essen)

⭐ How mastery works

  • 3 correct answers in a row → almost mastered 🌓
  • Correct again on a different day → mastered ⭐
  • A wrong answer resets words you are still learning
  • Almost-mastered words never go back to the start. A mastered word that you miss drops to almost mastered and needs just one correct answer.

🧩 Compound nouns

The last part decides: das Zimmer → das Wohnzimmer, der Tisch → der Schreibtisch.
These are tendencies, not laws — there are exceptions (der Moment, das Auge…). The tips in the game only appear when they match.
